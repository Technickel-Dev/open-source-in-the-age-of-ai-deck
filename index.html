Where this started

Hacktoberfest got a lot of us
into Open Source

  1. 2014

    Four PRs, one t-shirt

    DigitalOcean launches Hacktoberfest: open four pull requests in October and earn a shirt.

  2. 2015–25

    The on-ramp, then the flood

    Thousands make a first contribution. Then maintainers drown in low-effort PRs, now easier than ever to generate with AI.

  3. 2026

    The scoreboard goes away

    MLH and DEV take over, and pull requests no longer count toward rewards.

Contributing in the age of AI

Don't be the reason PRs stopped counting

  1. 01

    Read the rules

    CONTRIBUTING.md, and the project's AI policy if it has one.

  2. 02

    Talk first

    Comment on the issue before you open a PR.

  3. 03

    Own every line

    If AI wrote it, you reviewed it, tested it and can explain it.

  4. 04

    Quality over quantity

    One thoughtful PR beats ten drive-bys. Disclose AI help when asked.
